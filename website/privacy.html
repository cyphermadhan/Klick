Privacy Policy

The short version

Klick doesn't have a server, so there's nothing of yours for us to collect, store, or sell. There is no account, no sign-up, and no analytics SDK in the app. Everything the app needs — your device name, your encryption keys, your channels and peers — is generated on your iPhone and stays on your iPhone.

Data we collect

Klick's developer collects none of your data. The app has no backend, no database, and no analytics or crash-reporting service embedded in it. We never see your messages, your voice, your contacts, or your location.

  • No account or identity. There's no sign-up, login, phone number, or email required to use Klick.
  • No servers. Klick talks directly, device-to-device, over WiFi or Bluetooth — nothing is routed through infrastructure we operate.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no third-party SDK collecting usage data, advertising identifiers, or behavioral analytics.
  • No advertising. Klick shows no ads and shares no data with ad networks.

Data generated and stored on your device

To function, Klick creates and stores the following, entirely on your device, using the iOS Keychain and local app storage:

  • Encryption keys — generated on-device when you pair with someone or create a channel. Keys are 32-byte libsodium keys and never leave your device unencrypted.
  • Device name — a label you choose, shown to peers you connect with. It's not tied to any real-world identity unless you make it one.
  • Channels and peer list — the names and keys of channels you've created or joined, and the peers you've paired with.
  • Chat history — text and Morse messages you've sent or received, kept locally in the app.

None of the above is transmitted to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app, or using the unpair/reset options in Settings, removes it from your device.

Permissions Klick requests, and why

iOS will prompt you for the following permissions. Each is used only for the feature named — none of them enable any data collection by us.

  • Bluetooth — to discover nearby phones when WiFi isn't available, and to pair with an optional Meshtastic LoRa radio for long-range text.
  • Local network — to discover and communicate with nearby Klick devices over WiFi.
  • Microphone — for push-to-talk voice transmission, and for decoding Morse code picked up from another device's speaker.
  • Camera — to scan pairing/invite QR codes, and to decode Morse code flashed from another phone's torch.

What Apple sees

If you install Klick through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple collects standard distribution and crash diagnostics as part of operating those platforms. That data is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one — we don't have access to it beyond what App Store Connect surfaces to any developer.

Third-party sharing

We don't share, sell, or rent any data, because we don't collect any in the first place. Klick has no third-party integrations that transmit your information off your device.

Children's privacy

Klick is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age — there's no account system to collect information through.

Changes to this policy

If Klick's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds an optional server-backed feature — this page will be updated first, and the "last updated" date above will reflect it.

Contact

Klick doesn't have a support inbox yet. Questions or concerns can be filed as an issue on GitHub.
